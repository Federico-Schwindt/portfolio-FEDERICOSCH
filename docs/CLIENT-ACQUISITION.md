# Getting clients: the playbook

The website now captures leads (free audit, contact form, pricing buttons) into Neon. This file is the part a website cannot do on its own: getting the right people to it. Do the first week once, then the weekly routine.

## Week 1: foundations (about 4 hours)

1. **Google Search Console.** Add the property `https://federico-schwindt.github.io/portfolio-FEDERICOSCH/`, verify it with the HTML-tag method, and submit `sitemap.xml`. Request indexing for the home page and `/work`.
2. **Custom domain.** Buy `federicoschwindt.com` (or `zyneteq.com`) and point it at GitHub Pages. A real domain ranks better, looks more serious in emails, and makes `robots.txt` work. Update `SITE_URL` in `src/lib/seo.ts` and the workflow when you do.
3. **Google Business Profile** as a "Website designer" service-area business in Gualeguaychú. Add the site, the three packages as products, and photos of the projects. Ask the five Freelancer clients who left 5-star reviews to also leave one on Google.
4. **Profiles that link back** (each one is a backlink and a lead source): LinkedIn (featured section: the site and two case studies), Behance and Dribbble (upload the Velocity Motors, Atelier Norte and Brasa Coffee concepts), Clutch, GoodFirms, Upwork, Workana (Latin America), Fiverr Pro.
5. **Freelancer.com profile.** Add the site link, the new pricing and the concept pieces to the portfolio.

## Weekly routine (about 5 hours a week)

| Day | Action | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | Bid on Freelancer / Upwork / Workana jobs that match a case study | 10 bids |
| Tue | Send personalised audit offers to local businesses with weak sites | 15 emails |
| Wed | Publish one LinkedIn post (a before/after, a result, a concept film) | 1 post |
| Thu | Follow up everyone from last week who did not reply | all |
| Fri | Check `/admin`, answer every lead, mark status, review what converted | inbox zero |

## Who to target first

Your case studies already prove these niches, so pitch them by name:

- **Car dealers and rental companies:** Certified Retail Cars, Sure Thing, Velocity Motors concept.
- **Clinics, spas and wellness:** Mirage TLV, Nova Dental concept.
- **Architecture, construction and interior finishing:** Minrat Projects, Atelier Norte concept.
- **Online stores (WooCommerce/Shopify):** Universal Driveshafts (+551%), Joya Hijab, DogStore, UltraMedix.

Search Google Maps for these categories in the US, Spain, Israel and Argentina, open their websites, and keep the ones that are slow, not mobile-friendly or have no clear call to action.

## Templates

**Audit offer (English).** Subject: *Quick idea for {Business} website*

> Hi {Name}, I was looking at {business}.com and noticed {one specific problem: e.g. the inventory page takes 9 seconds to load on a phone}. I build websites for {niche}: last year a redesign I did grew a parts retailer's sales by 551%. I recorded a short free audit with the three changes I would make first. Want me to send it over? — Federico, federico-schwindt.github.io/portfolio-FEDERICOSCH

**Oferta de auditoría (español).** Asunto: *Una idea rápida para la web de {Negocio}*

> Hola {Nombre}, estuve mirando {negocio}.com y vi que {un problema concreto}. Diseño y desarrollo sitios para {rubro}; un rediseño reciente aumentó las ventas de una tienda un 551%. Te preparé una auditoría gratuita con los tres cambios que haría primero. ¿Te la envío? — Federico

**Freelancer / Upwork bid opener.**

> I have built exactly this before: {matching case study + link}. For your project I would {two concrete steps}. I can start {day} and deliver the first version in {n} days. Fixed price: {price}, with monthly care from ${monthly}.

## What to measure

Leads per week by source (the `source` column: audit, contact, pricing), reply rate to outreach, and leads → calls → clients. Double down on the channel that turns into calls, not the one that gets likes.
